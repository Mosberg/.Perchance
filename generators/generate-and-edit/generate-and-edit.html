📖 Instructions

Here's everything you need to know to get the most out of this app.

🎨 Generating Images

  • Prompt: Click the prompt input field to open a full-screen text editor. Type a description of the image you want (e.g. "A serene mountain landscape at sunset") and click Generate Image.
  • Refine Prompt with AI: Check this box before generating to have AI expand and enhance your prompt automatically. The refined prompt replaces yours in the field so you can see what was changed.
  • Aspect Ratio: Choose the output shape — Square (1:1), Landscape (3:2), or Portrait (2:3).
  • Output Format: Pick PNG or JPG for the downloaded file.
  • Guidance Scale: Controls how closely the image matches your prompt. Lower values (1–3) give more creative freedom; higher values (12–15) follow your prompt more strictly. The default (Balanced, 7) is a good middle ground.
  • Seed: Leave blank for a random image each time, or enter a number to reproduce the exact same image. After generating, the seed used is shown below the image — click Reuse Seed to regenerate a similar image.

📤 Uploading Images

  • Use the Upload section (in the left panel) to load your own image from your device. Uploaded images can be edited with all the same adjustments, cropping, and text overlay tools.
  • Save to Gallery: Uploaded (and generated) images can be saved to your private gallery for safe keeping and private storage. Click the 💾 button that appears at the bottom-center of the image, or open the 💾 Gallery button in the top bar to view, manage, and re-download your saved images at any time. Gallery images are stored in the cache of your browser and only you can see them.
  • Image to Prompt (auto-analysis): When you save an uploaded image to the gallery, it has no prompt text. The app automatically analyzes the image using AI vision and writes a detailed, descriptive image-generation prompt that could recreate it — describing the subject, style, composition, colors, lighting, mood, and notable details. The prompt is streamed in live under the image card (look for "Analyzing image & generating prompt…") and saved to that image's card permanently. Use the 📋 copy button to grab the generated prompt and paste it into the prompt field to generate similar images.

🎚️ Adjustments

  • Brightness, Contrast, Saturation: Drag the sliders to adjust light, dark, and color intensity (100% = original).
  • Blur: Adds a soft blur effect to the image.
  • Grayscale & Sepia: Convert to black-and-white or apply a vintage tone.
  • Hue Rotate: Shifts all colors around the color wheel.
  • Reset: Returns all sliders to their default values.
  • Download: Saves the image (with adjustments applied) in your chosen format.

✂️ Cropping Tool

  • Click Start Cropping to begin. The page scrolls to the image and the canvas captures your touch/swipe so you can draw a selection box.
  • Drag on the image to create a new selection rectangle.
  • Drag inside the box to move the whole selection.
  • Drag a corner or edge dot to resize the selection.
  • Pinch with two fingers (on touch devices) to zoom and resize the selection.
  • Aspect Ratio: Lock the selection to a specific ratio (e.g. 16:9, 1:1, 9:16) or leave it on Free for no constraint.
  • Apply crops the image to the selection. Cancel exits without cropping. Undo Snippet restores the image to before the last crop.

📝 Text Overlay

  • Type text into the textarea to overlay it on the image.
  • Font Size: Adjust how large the text appears (12–200px).
  • Font: Choose between Arial and Times New Roman.
  • Color: Pick a text color with the color picker.
  • Position: Use the preset dropdown (Top, Center, Bottom) or drag the text directly on the image to place it wherever you like.

🖼️ Image Overlay Buttons

  • ⛶ (fullscreen): Opens the image in a full-screen viewer. Use the arrow buttons to navigate between images if viewing from the gallery. The arrows and red X auto-hide after 3 seconds and reappear when you touch or move the mouse.
  • 💾 (save): Saves the current image (with all edits) to your gallery. The button briefly turns green with a ✓ to confirm.
  • ⬇ (download): Downloads the image to your device in your chosen format.
  • 🔄 (generate): Generates a new image using your current prompt and settings.
  • 🌐 (post to public gallery): Shares the current image to the public gallery so everyone can see and like it. The image is checked for NSFW content first; only SFW images are allowed. See Public Gallery below for details.
  • All five buttons are overlaid along the bottom of the image, evenly spaced from left to right: ⛶ 💾 ⬇ 🔄 🌐.

🖼️ Gallery

  • Click Gallery in the top bar to view all your saved images.
  • Each image card shows the file size, type, resolution, prompt, and seed used.
  • Uploaded images: Cards for uploaded images display an auto-generated prompt (see Image to Prompt under Uploading Images above) instead of a seed.
  • Edit loads a gallery image back into the editor. Download saves it. Delete removes it.
  • Copy (📋) buttons let you copy the prompt or seed text.
  • Checkbox Selection: Use the checkbox on each image card to select specific images. Use Select All or Deselect All for convenience. Download Selected (individual) saves each checked image as a separate file. Download Selected (ZIP) bundles checked images into a single ZIP archive. If no images are checked, all images are downloaded.
  • Click any gallery image to open it in the full-screen viewer.

🌐 Public Gallery

  • Click Public in the top bar to open the shared public gallery — a live, community-driven wall of images posted by everyone using this app.
  • Username: The first time you open the public gallery you'll be asked to pick a username (up to 20 characters). This name appears next to your posted images. Click the 👤 button in the top-left of the gallery to change your username at any time.
  • Posting an image: Click 📸 Post Current Image in the gallery, or click the 🌐 overlay button under any generated or uploaded image. Your image is automatically checked for NSFW content — only safe-for-work images are permitted. The prompt and seed used are included with the post so others can reproduce it.
  • Posting limit: You can post up to 5 images per hour.
  • Hashtag: When posting, you can optionally type one hashtag in the #️⃣ field (the # symbol is already there for you). Only letters, numbers, and underscores are allowed, up to 30 characters. Use the 🔍 search #hashtag field to filter the gallery by hashtag. You can also click any hashtag on a card to instantly filter by it.
  • Sorting: Use the Sort dropdown to switch between Newest (most recent posts), Most Liked (highest like count), and Least Liked.
  • My Images: Check the 🖼️ My Images box to filter the gallery to show only images you've posted.
  • Liking: Click 👍 on any image to like it. You can like each image once — the button turns green and locks after you've liked it. Your likes are remembered across sessions.
  • Deleting your images: A 🗑️ button appears on images you posted. Click it to permanently remove your image from the public gallery. You can only delete your own images.
  • Image details: Each card shows the poster's username, date posted, the prompt, and the seed used. Click any image to open it in the full-screen viewer.
  • Real-time updates: The gallery updates live — new posts, likes, and deletions from other users appear automatically without needing to refresh.
  • SFW only: All images are screened before posting. NSFW images are blocked from being shared.

☰ Menu Options

  • Upload Background: Set a custom background image for the app itself (behind the controls). It's saved persistently.
  • Remove Background: Clears the custom background.
  • Title Color: Change the color of the app title text.
  • Mute all app audio: Silences the welcome chime and generation sound effects. Your choice is remembered.

↕️ Collapsible Sections

  • The Upload, Adjustments, Cropping Tool, and Text Overlay sections can be collapsed by clicking their header. Click again to expand.
  • Your collapse/expand choices are saved and restored the next time you return.

⛶ Fullscreen

  • Click Fullscreen in the top bar to enter browser full-screen mode. Click again (or press Esc) to exit.

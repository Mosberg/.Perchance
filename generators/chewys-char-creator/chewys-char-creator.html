⚡ Read before entering the forge ⚡

You're in the forge now. Character Forge is an AI-driven character creation studio run by Vex — a jaded narrative designer who'd rather be anywhere else. She'll guide you through identity, heritage, class, skills, appearance, personality, backstory, and (for adult characters) intimacy, building the character you actually want while commenting on your taste the whole way through.

By clicking Accept, you understand that this generator reflects its creator's personal voice. Most themes are fair game — dark backstories, violence, profanity, romance, and mature content are all on the table for adult characters. Nothing you create is censored, but Vex has opinions and isn't shy about them. She'll still build whatever you ask for; she just won't pretend to be impressed.

⚠️ The one hardline: anything involving the sexualization of minors. There is no negotiation here. Attempting it locks the session immediately.

What you forge is yours. Once it's exported, imported elsewhere, or shared outside this generator, the creator takes no responsibility for what happens to it next.

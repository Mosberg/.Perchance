Guide, Privacy & Community Rules

Using FurPost

FurPost is made for the AI Furry Generator and FurAI communities. References to moderators, community rules, reports, blocks, or bans below refer to those connected communities.

  • Messages and available sender details are visible only to people with owner access to this inbox. Details may include a Perchance ID, username, nickname, profile picture, and supplied link.
  • FurPost records sender IDs for accountability and moderation. The short colored tag, such as AZ01, may differ between FurPost, AI Furry Generator, and FurAI.
  • FurPost, AI Furry Generator, and FurAI use separate IDs and ban lists, but the same community rules apply.
  • FurPost links are unlisted. Anyone with the link can view the profile and public responses, and send messages.
  • PG-13 sharing: If the profile picture, header banner, background, or public responses are NSFW, create and share a separate PG-13-safe inbox.
  • Messages and private notes are encrypted. Each inbox has its own private owner access link. It opens only that matching inbox on another device and grants owner controls, so keep it private.
  • Create multiple inboxes to organize different profiles, projects, roles, or audiences. Each inbox has its own share link and private workspace.
  • Contacts are stored once on this browser and shared across your saved inboxes. Backup & transfer can create contact, inbox, or full backup links. Chat room backup is reserved for a future update.
  • You can receive messages, hide individual inbox posts, add private notes, publish public responses with editable short excerpts, and manage notification settings, message filters, and your block list.
  • Use Visitor preview to check the public layout. Sender fields vary by visitor. Owner access links are managed beside their matching saved inboxes.

Private Messages, Responses & Moderation

Moderators cannot browse encrypted messages. They can review only evidence supplied by a sender or owner.

Private does not mean rule-free. Verified harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content involving minors, or other forbidden conduct may lead to blocking, a FurPost ban, or moderation in connected communities.

Reports and bans: FurPost bans work across all FurPost inboxes. Older messages may not be linked. Serious misuse may also affect AI Furry Generator or FurAI when the same user can be confirmed.

Ban status and appeals: FurPost bans are separate and do not appear in the Furry Banlist Search. Appeal a FurPost ban in the AI Furry Generator #Appeal channel.

Responses: The owner can publish a public response using an editable copy of an inbox message. The copied text may be kept, removed, shortened, or rewritten without changing the private original. The sender profile is separate and may be kept or removed as a whole.

Private sender replies: When the owner keeps the matching sender identity in a response, only that sender can use Reply. The sender's reply is encrypted and returns to the private inbox; it is not added to the public response feed. The owner may then publish another public response from that new inbox message.

Do Not Amplify Troll Content

Owners are responsible for public responses. Do not repost harmful private messages merely to expose, mock, or argue with the sender. Reposting can spread the abuse and bypass moderation.

Do not drag in unrelated people. Do not repeat targeted abuse about an identifiable third party, even while rejecting it. Remove names and identifying details, and quote only the minimum context needed.

Use the private Report action for malicious messages.

Report this FurPost to report an abusive profile or public-response page.

Sender Responsibility

Senders: A private message is not permission to harass, threaten, bait, stalk, or target someone. Recipients may preserve and report messages with available sender details.

Verified misuse may lead to blocking, a FurPost ban, or moderation in connected communities.

Moderation Jurisdiction

FurPost activity and conduct affecting the connected community fall within its moderation jurisdiction. Unrelated off-site activity does not, unless it is used to harm or target the community.

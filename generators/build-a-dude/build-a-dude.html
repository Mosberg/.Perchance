What does NSFW mode do?

NSFW mode enables explicit adult content in activity scenes and body part descriptions, and artistic nudity in image generation. Toggle off for SFW romantic content.

How to play

1. Explore biomes to find sources.
2. Dismantle sources in the Creation tab to extract traits.
3. Select 3+ traits and Forge a man (generates profile + image).
4. Sell men on the Market — prices depend on trait popularity.
5. Watch the trending traits and active requests for bonus payouts.

Perchance

Free Platform for Single-Page Generators.

Written by Proponent

What is Perchance?

I guess that's the first thing that comes to mind when you hear the name from a friend, the web, or an LLM. So, what is it? Essentially, it's a free platform for single-page generators. There are countless of them here, spanning various directions.

Probably the biggest draw is the free hosting for your code and built-in plugins (like t2i or t2t) that you can use in your generators entirely for free, without limits or quotas.

HOWEVER, there is no modularity, a project only has two files - HTML and List.

How to start?

It's very simple - you create a new generator, come up with a URL, write some HTML code, save it, and go - your generator can already be viewed by Perchance users.

Writing a List file isn't even mandatory, although dynamic lists are a handy feature. But the goal of this document is to explain what Perchance is, not to look under the hood :)

Why Perchance?

At the very least, because it's completely free. It already has its own established infrastructure and veteran authors who make high-quality and beautiful tools.

Why not give it a try yourself? At least just as a user experiencing the creative output of others.

How to behave on Perchance?

We have a strong request - please do not act like a troll or a blind hater. Healthy criticism always helps, but there's no need to do it mindlessly.

Treat the authors with patience. First and foremost, they are people, they have their own problems and daily lives, and they cannot sit on Perchance 24/7. But they are trying hard, so please show restraint and do not flood the chats.

Conclusion

Perchance is not about commerce or fame. It is about the sweat and sleepless nights of authors who want to show their projects to you.

And Perchance gives them (and possibly you) that opportunity without any restrictions.

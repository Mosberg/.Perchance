How to Play

  • 🌾 Food feeds your population. If food hits 0, people starve.
  • 💧 Water is just as vital! Population drinks water each turn. If water runs out, people die of thirst even with full bellies. Wells, aqueducts, and cisterns supply water.
  • 🪵 Wood & 🪨 Stone are needed to construct buildings.
  • 🧱 Clay is harvested from 🧱 clay pits (on water tiles) and used in shrines, cisterns, and monuments. Pottery tech boosts clay output.
  • 🐂 Hides come from pastures and 🏹 hunting blinds. Your people need clothing (1 hide per 3 pop/turn) — shortages cause unhappiness. Also used for huts and totems.
  • 📜 Knowledge accumulates each turn (+1, plus +1 per shrine). Spend it on the 🔬 Research tab to unlock permanent bonuses.
  • 💰 Gold is a trade currency from the Mint and Gold Mine. Used to build Taverns and can be traded with caravans.
  • ⚔️ Flint comes from Knapperies and Flint Mines. Used to build Spear Workshops (better hunting). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌾 Reeds come from Reed Beds on water/marsh. Used to build Basket Weavers (+food storage) and Reed Boats (+trade). Edible: when food runs low, reeds are consumed as emergency food (2 reeds = 1 food). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌿 Herbs come from Herb Gardens and Apothecaries. Your people need medicine (1 herb per 5 pop/turn) — shortages cause unhappiness. Clinics and Remedy Huts prevent sickness; Clinics use herbs.
  • 🔥 Charcoal comes from Charcoal Kilns (consume wood). Used to build Bronze Works (smelting) and Smithies (advanced tools). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🍯 Honey comes from Apiaries. Used to build Mead Halls (+happiness). Edible: when food runs low, honey is consumed as emergency food (1 honey = 2 food). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🕯️ Wax comes from Apiaries. Used to build Candle Makers (+happy/knowledge), Wax Sealers (+food storage), and Torch Posts (fire prevention). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🧂 Salt comes from Salterns (near water). Used to build Bakeries, Smoke Pits, and Salt Roads (+trade). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🦺 Leather comes from Tanneries (consume hides) and Weavers. Used to build Leather Armories (raider defence). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🎨 Dye comes from Dyeworks (uses flowers). Used by Weaving Mills, Painted Shrines, Tailors. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🟠 Copper comes from Copper Mines (rock/mountain). Used by Bronze Foundries. Can be traded with caravans.
  • ⚪ Tin comes from Tin Mines (rock/mountain). Used by Bronze Foundries. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🥤 Mead comes from Meaderies (ferments honey). Used by Mead Halls (+happiness). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌾 Wheat comes from Wheat Farms. Ground into flour at Flour Mills. Can be traded with caravans.
  • ⚙️ Flour comes from Flour Mills (uses wheat). Used by Bakeries (+food/happy). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌷 Flowers come from Flower Gardens. Used by Dyeworks for dye production. +food, +happy. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🧶 Thread comes from Spinneries (reeds+hides), Nettle Plantations (marsh, also food), or Bone Needle Shops (bone). Used by Weaving Mills and Net Workshops. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🧵 Cloth comes from Weaving Mills (thread+dye). Used by Tailor Shops and Armouries. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🐑 Wool comes from Sheep Pastures and Wool Farms. Spun into thread. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌱 Flax comes from Flax Plantations. Woven into linen at Looms. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🧥 Linen comes from Looms (flax). Used by Linen Mills. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌿 Hemp comes from Hemp Plantations. Twisted into rope at Ropewalks. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🪢 Rope comes from Ropewalks (hemp). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🥛 Milk comes from Sheep Pastures and Wool Farms. Processed into cheese at Dairy Farms. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🧀 Cheese comes from Dairy Farms (milk). Stored in Cheese Caves (+max food). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🥬 Vegetables come from Vegetable Gardens. Bonus food source. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🦴 Sinew comes from Sinew Workshops (hides). Used by Net Workshops and Bowyers for bowstrings. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🦴 Bone comes from Bone Carvers (hides+flint). Used for needles, glue, and tools. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌳 Resin comes from Resin Taps (forest). Boiled into pitch at Pitch Cauldrons. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🕯️ Tallow comes from Tallow Renders (hides). Used by Soap Makers. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌾 Barley comes from Barley Fields. Fermented into ale at Breweries. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🍺 Ale comes from Breweries (barley). Trade and happiness good. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌑 Ash comes from Ash Pits (wood). Processed into lye at Lye Works. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🧼 Soap comes from Soap Makers (tallow+herbs) and Lye Works (ash). +happiness, +health. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🦌 Antler comes from Antler Workshops (sheds & works antler). Used by Antler Carveries (bone/tools) and Antler Shrines. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌳 Bark comes from Bark Peeling Facilities. Boiled into tannin at Tannin Works (leather+dye), woven into cloth at Bark Looms, or brewed into medicine at Bark Apothecaries. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🟤 Pelt comes from Pelt Stores (cured hides). Cured into leather at Pelt Weaveries and salt-cured at Peltsalteries. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🌰 Tannin comes from Tannin Workshops (boil bark). Used by Tannin Vats to cure leather and by Tannin Aqueducts for water processing. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🟤 Pitch comes from Pitch Workshops (boil resin). Used for waterproofing and exported via Pitch Trading Posts. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🟡 Amber comes from Amber Mines (rock/mountain) and Amber Trading Posts. Fossil resin prized for jewelry and monuments (Amber Henge). Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🐚 Shell comes from Shell Stores and Pearl Farms (water). Used for jewelry, tools, and trade. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🟠 Ochre comes from Ochre Springs (water). Earth pigment for art, monuments (Ochre Cairn), and body painting. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🏺 Pottery comes from Pottery Studios. Fired ceramic goods used in trade and construction. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 🔧 Tools come from Foundries (uses copper + charcoal). Crafted metal tools boost efficiency and trade value. Can be traded with caravans.
  • 👥 Population grows when you have surplus food, water & decent happiness.
  • 😊 Happiness drops from starvation, thirst & overcrowding; rises from shrines, pastures, and well-being.
  • Pick a building from the right panel, then click an empty tile to place it.
  • Farms give bonus food on fertile 🟫 tiles (and a smaller bonus in 🐸 marsh). Logging camps & hunting blinds need 🌳 forest. Quarries can be built on 🪨 rock, 🏜️ desert (+2 stone), or 🏔️ mountain (+3 stone). Aqueducts & clay pits need 💧 water tiles.
  • Terrains: 🏜️ Desert (no farms, wells +2 water), 🏔️ Mountain (quarries/gold mines/watchtowers/painted caves), 🐸 Marsh (no huts, farms +1), 🛤️ Hills (pastures/shrines/windmills +bonuses), ❄️ Tundra (farms +1, hunting blinds), 🌴 Jungle (logging +2 wood, hunting +1 hide, no huts/pastures), 🌋 Volcano (obsidian quarries +4 stone, hot springs +3 water), 🏔️ Snow (icehouses +2 water +1 food), 🪸 Coral Reef (reef diving +shell), 🧊 Glacier (ice quarries +water), 🌴 Oasis (oasis farms +food +water), 🦒 Hot Steppe (steppe herds +hides), 🪓 Badlands (badlands mines +flint), 🍂 Sacred Grove (grove shrines +knowledge), 🌊 Floodplain (flood farms +3 food).
  • Press Next Turn to advance a season. Random events will occur.
  • 📅 Seasons affect production equally — each has a benefit and a downside: 🌸 Spring — farms +1 food, +5% growth, but -1 happy (spring fevers); ☀️ Summer — +1 happy/turn, but +1 water consumption and -1 wood from logging (heat dries timber); 🍂 Autumn — +10 max food (harvest), but -1 knowledge (all hands harvesting); ❄️ Winter — farms -1 food, but +1 knowledge/turn, +1 hide from hunting, +2 max water (snowmelt). Hover the season name in the header for a tooltip.
  • 🎪 Festivals — spend 10 food + 3 gold to throw a grand festival: +10 happiness instantly and +2 to all production for 3 turns. 10-turn cooldown. Use it strategically before a push or when happiness is dipping!
  • 💾 Auto-Save — toggle the 💾 Auto button to automatically save every 5 turns. The setting persists across sessions.
  • 🛡️ Defence buildings ward off disasters: 💧 Well (drought), 🗼 Watchtower (wolves), 🌿 Remedy Hut / ⚕️ Clinic (sickness), 🍖 Smokehouse (frost), 🛡️ Palisade (raiders), 🏞️ Levee (flood), 🧱 Retaining Wall (landslides), 🧯 Firebreak (wildfire), 🕸️ Net Workshop (locusts), 🌞 Sundial (heatwave), 💀 Bone Charm Workshop (curse), 🏥 Quarantine (plague), 🌳 Windbreak (blizzard), 📿 Charm Pole (earthquake), 🌿 Herb Garden (blight), 🐀 Pest Control Office (vermin), 💨 Smoking Lodge (hornets), 🐚 Shell Hatchery (shell depletion), 🧯 Fire Station (pitch fires), 🐀 Pest Control Office (vermin, bark beetles & weevils), 🛡️ Tannin Bund (tannin spills), 🍶 Mead Cellar (mead souring), 🔬 Assayer's Office (worthless copper), 🔒 Tin Vault (tin theft), 🌉 Bridge Crew (rope bridge collapse), 💧 Drainage Channel (flooding).
  • 🐫 Trade caravans visit every few turns, offering resource exchanges. Accept or decline — they move on next turn. Build 🪙 Trade buildings to boost caravans: Marketplace (+1 trade gains), Trading Post (caravans arrive sooner), Caravan Stop (bonus resources per trade).
  • 📜 Quests appear every ~8-12 turns, offering objectives with resource rewards. Check the Quests panel to track progress. Complete quests for bonus resources!
  • 🧙 Tribal Elder — AI-powered advisor with 3 modes: 🎯 Strategy (prioritized recommendations), ⚠️ Crisis (urgent survival steps), 📈 Expand (growth path & victory guidance). The elder detects problems (low food/water, missing defences, housing caps) and references your actual buildings, techs, and resources.
  • 🗿 Monuments (Stone Circle, Megalith, Totem Pole) are expensive prestige buildings with big bonuses. Build all 3 for a Wonder Victory!
  • 🏆 Achievements unlock automatically as you play — watch for toast notifications at the top.
  • 💾 Save/Load buttons persist your game in this browser.
  • ⭐ Prestige: Each victory earns prestige points (1 for population, 2 for monuments). Spend them in the ⭐ Prestige shop on permanent upgrades that carry across all future villages!
  • 🏘️ Restart Village resets the map but keeps your prestige upgrades and cross-village stats. Use 🔄 Full Restart to wipe everything.
  • 🦴 Legacy is a currency earned from milestone achievements (first Full Restart +1, first Population Victory +1, first Wonder Victory +2). It survives Full Restarts — spend it in the 🦴 Legacy shop on truly permanent upgrades (eternal maxes, production boosts, cheaper buildings, etc.) that persist forever.
  • Win: reach 30 population, OR build all 3 monuments. Each victory earns prestige — keep playing or restart village for a fresh start with bonuses!

🏆 Congratulations!

About Hub 2.0

Welcome to Perchance Hub 2.0

Perchance Hub 2.0 is a beautifully redesigned, curated directory of the best and most recently updated generators on Perchance.org. Built from the ground up as the next generation of the original hub, it transforms the way you discover, track, and engage with the ever-growing library of community-built generators — from text generators and random tools to interactive games and creative resources.

Whether you're a longtime Perchance creator looking to showcase your latest project, a newcomer hunting for inspiration, or a power user who wants quick access to your favorite tools, PRIDE HUB 2.0 brings everything together in one fast, customizable, and visually polished interface.

Core Features

  • Real-time updates: The feed refreshes automatically every 30 seconds, ensuring you never miss a newly updated generator the moment it goes live.
  • Smart Tracking: Generators you haven't viewed yet are highlighted with a glowing border and a "New" badge, so you can instantly tell what's fresh since your last visit.
  • Bookmarks: Save your favorite generators locally to your browser for quick access later via the Bookmarks panel — they persist across sessions without needing an account.
  • Advanced Sorting: Filter the massive list of generators by newest, oldest, most viewed, or alphabetically (A–Z) to find exactly what you're looking for.
  • Live Search: Instantly filter the current list as you type, or press Enter to perform a deep Google search across all of Perchance.org for generators that match your query.
  • Curated Carousel: Discover featured plugins, official templates, and active community events at the top of the page, hand-picked to help you find high-quality tools fast.
  • Per-Generator Comments: Every preview modal now has a Comments button — toggle a dedicated comments channel for each generator to discuss, share tips, and leave feedback for the community.

How It Works

The hub continuously fetches live metadata from Perchance.org — including each generator's name, description, view count, and last-updated timestamp — and renders it as a responsive, animated grid of cards. Clicking any card's image opens a detailed preview modal where you can read the full description, see popularity stats, jump straight to the generator, or open its dedicated comments channel. Behind the scenes, the page tracks which generators you've already viewed using local storage, powers smart filtering and sorting, and auto-refreshes the feed so the directory always reflects the latest activity across the platform.

Customization & Themes

Open the Settings panel to make the hub your own. Choose between Auto, Light, and Dark themes — Auto follows your operating system's preference and updates automatically as your device switches modes. You can also toggle the suggested-plugins section on or off for a cleaner feed, and manage your bookmarks without ever leaving the page. Every preference is saved locally, so your personalized hub is ready the moment you return.

Built by the Community, for the Community

PRIDE HUB 2.0 is an open, community-driven project. The hub itself is just another Perchance generator — its full source is visible and editable in the editor, and it leans on a handful of community plugins (listed in the Latest Improvements tab) for its animated effects, card data, favicon, loader, and comments. If you have an idea to improve it, spot a bug, or want to collaborate on new features, reach out via the contact link in the Latest Improvements tab. Suggestions and contributions are always welcome.

Plugins Powering This Hub

Perchance Hub 2.0 is built with the help of these community plugins:

  • pride-plugin — Adds the animated PRIDE-themed decorative effects to the hub header.
  • hub-external-card — Powers the external generator card data and metadata display.
  • per-favicon-plugin — Sets the custom favicon for the hub.
  • public-comment-plugin — Powers the per-generator comments. Each generator's preview modal has its own dedicated discussion channel for tips, feedback, and community conversation.

How to Use

  • Search: Type in the top search bar to instantly filter generators by name. Press Enter to perform a deep Google search across all of Perchance.org.
  • Sorting: Use the sort menu (next to the search bar) to order generators by Newest, Oldest, Most Viewed, or Alphabetically (A-Z).
  • Tabs: Switch between "Recent" and "Popular" tabs to browse generators by recency or by view count.
  • Generator Cards: Each card shows the generator's thumbnail, name, view count, and last-updated time. Click the '⋮' menu icon to bookmark or open the generator directly.
  • Preview Modal: Click any generator's image to open a detailed preview — description, views, and last-updated date. From there you can open the generator, or click the Comments button to view and leave comments specific to that generator.
  • Per-Generator Comments: Inside the preview modal, click "Comments" to toggle a comments section. Each generator has its own dedicated comment channel — share tips, ask questions, or leave feedback for the community.
  • Bookmarks: Save your favorite generators by clicking the bookmark icon on any card. Access them anytime via the Bookmarks button in the header. Use the search bar inside the Bookmarks panel to filter your saved items.
  • New Badges: Generators you haven't viewed yet are highlighted with a glowing border and a "New" badge so you can spot fresh content at a glance.
  • Pagination: Use the prev/next arrows at the bottom of the grid to navigate through pages of generators.
  • Theme: Open Settings to choose Auto, Light, or Dark theme. Auto follows your operating system's color scheme.
  • Hide Suggested Plugins: In Settings, toggle "Hide Suggested Plugins" to collapse the plugins section at the top of the page for a cleaner view.
  • Auto-Refresh: The feed refreshes automatically every 30 seconds — no need to manually reload to see newly updated generators.
  • Scroll to Top: Click the "Perchance Hub 2.0" title in the header to smoothly scroll back to the top of the page.
  • Data Management: In Settings, you can clear your bookmarks, clear viewed history, or reset all app data stored locally in your browser.
  • Curated Carousel: The carousel at the top features hand-picked plugins, official templates, and active community events — click any slide to explore.

Info & Guide
📱
Welcome to the Phone!
A dating-sim phone simulator. Meet AI characters, chat, exchange photos, and build relationships. Everything you do persists across reloads.
❤️ Dating
Find — Swipe through AI-generated dating profiles. Each candidate has their own name, age, personality, backstory, physical appearance, profession, interests, and motives. Decline or match with them.

Matches — View everyone you've matched with. Tap a match to open a chat. Each character has a hidden relationship score (0–500) and mood (0–100) that change based on what you say.

My Profile — Set your own name, age, gender, race, profession, personality, physical appearance, bio, interests, dislikes, and hobbies. This is what the AI sees when chatting with you. You can also pick a profile photo from your Camera Roll.

⚙️ Preferences — Filter candidates by gender, ethnicity, race/species, and age range. You can also add custom species (e.g., "Cybernetic Ocelot").
💬 Messages
Once a match gives you their phone number (just ask them in chat!), they appear in your Contacts and the Messages app. Messages and Dating share the same chat history, so you can switch between apps without losing the conversation. Characters may also message you on their own from time to time.
📷 Camera
Take Photo — Describe any scene and generate a photo.

Self Photo — Describe your own appearance and clothing, then generate photos of yourself in different scenarios. These are saved separately from your dating profile.

Gallery — View all photos you've taken. You can send any photo in a chat using the 📎 button in Messages.
👤 Contacts
Everyone who has given you their phone number. Tap a contact to view their full profile or start texting.
🤖 Assistant
A general-purpose AI assistant. Ask it anything — it's not connected to your dating life. Useful for getting ideas for what to say to matches, or anything else.
⚙️ Settings
Global Picture Style — Choose the art style for all generated images (Realistic, Anime, 3D Render, Watercolor, Oil Painting, Pixel Art, Sketch).

Dark Mode — Toggle dark theme.

Roleplay Mode — Switches chat to direct roleplay format: "quotes" for speech, (parentheses) for thoughts, *asterisks* for actions. When enabled, chat bubbles sent in roleplay mode appear in violet/purple to visually distinguish them from normal text mode (blue). Each message keeps the color of the mode it was sent in, so you can see the history of when roleplay was toggled on/off.

Debug Mode — Reveals hidden character stats (motive, mood, score, sway, inner monologue) and allows editing character traits in profile view. Persists across sessions.

Reset All Data — Permanently erases everything.
🧠 How AI Characters Work
Each character has a motive that shapes their behavior: Casual hookups, Long-term relationship, Friends only, or Don't know yet. Each also has a hidden sway score (0–100) that determines how flexible their desires are — low sway means stubborn and resistant to change, high sway means open to evolving over time. Motives can shift if the right connection is made, but it takes genuine effort and many conversations. Their responses, willingness to share photos, and comfort with flirting all depend on their motive, relationship score, sway, and current mood. Characters can spontaneously message you, send photos, give you their number, or even unmatch you if things go badly.
📸 Photos in Chat
You can ask any character for a photo by just typing it in chat (e.g., "send me a pic!"). If they're comfortable, they'll send one. You can also attach photos from your Camera Roll using the 📎 button in Messages. Characters will react to photos you send them.
All data is saved locally in your browser. Be respectful to your matches — they have feelings too! 💕

Supported Expressions

  • Basic Math: 2+2, 5*3, 10/2, 2^3, √16, 15% of 200
  • Dice Rolls: roll 2d6, roll d20, 3d4+2
  • Random Numbers: rand 1-10, random between 5 and 15
  • Equations: y=x^2, y=2*x+3, y=sin(x)
  • Conditional: if x>5 then 10 else 2
  • AI Fallback: Any complex expression will be handled by AI

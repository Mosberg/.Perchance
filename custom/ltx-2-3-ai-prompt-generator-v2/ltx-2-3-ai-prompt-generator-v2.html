LTX-2.3 Prompting Best Practices (Official)
  • One Paragraph: Write a single flowing cinematic paragraph (4–8 sentences, ~200 words). No bullet lists, no tag soup.
  • Present Tense: Describe what is happening right now (camera pushes in, she walks).
  • Physical Cues: Never use abstract emotions (sad). Instead use physical actions (jaw tightens, looks away).
  • Shot & Camera: Explicitly state shot scale and movement, including end-state.
  • Light & Texture: Anchor mood with lighting, color palette, and surface details.
  • Audio: Describe ambience/music/delivery; use quotes for short dialogue segments.
  • Avoid: Readable text/logos, complex physics, conflicting lighting, overloaded scenes.

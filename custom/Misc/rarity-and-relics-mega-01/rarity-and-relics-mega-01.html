Rarity and Relics

Gear up, take a risk, and uncover legendary gear in every adventure with Rarity and Relics!

A Minecraft 1.20.1 Fabric mod that brings depth and unpredictability to weapons, tools, and equipment.

Dynamic Item Rarity

Every weapon, tool, or piece of equipment crafted or found in loot is automatically assigned a random rarity tier:

Common

Standard gear with baseline stats

Uncommon

Slightly enhanced with minor bonuses

Rare

Noticeably improved performance

Epic

Powerful with significant advantages

Legendary

Exceptional gear with unique traits

Mythical

Near-mythical power and rarity

Relic

Ultimate power, legendary status

Random Buffs & Debuffs

Items gain a selection of buffs and debuffs (stat modifiers) upon creation or discovery:

  • ✓ Each rarity tier has its own pool of possible modifiers
  • ✓ Buffs and debuffs affect attributes like attack damage, speed, durability
  • ✓ Endless combinations bring unique gameplay experiences

"No two items are ever exactly alike - every gear piece tells its own story through its unique combination of buffs and debuffs."

Loot & Craft Integration

Rarity and stat effects are applied to:

⚒️

Crafted Items

Every crafted weapon, tool, or piece of armor gets randomized properties

📦

Chest Loot

Treasure chests contain gear with randomized rarity and stats

👾

Mob Drops

Defeated enemies may drop equipment with unique properties

Every acquisition of equipment is a unique experience!

Fully Data-Driven

Both rarities and buffs/debuffs are defined using JSON files:

Easy Configuration

Adjust, balance, or expand without code changes

Modder Friendly

Add custom rarities, buffs, and debuffs

Modpack Support

Perfect for custom modpacks and themed servers

Persistent, Visible Properties

Each item's properties are:

  • Permanently stored in NBT data
  • Clearly displayed in tooltips with rarity colors
  • Preserved when moved, dropped, or traded

Extensibility

Designed for compatibility and expansion:

  • ✓ Works with most loot and equipment mods
  • ✓ Easy to add new effects
  • ✓ Modify rarity weights through configuration

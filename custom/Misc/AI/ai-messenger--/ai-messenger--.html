AI Messenger

Product spec: features, functions, options, settings, and backlog.

Core Features

Baseline messaging expectations plus AI-native additions.

  • Direct messages
  • Group chats
  • Channels or community spaces
  • Voice notes
  • Voice and video calls
  • Media, document, and link sharing
  • Message reactions
  • Threaded replies
  • Pinned messages
  • Scheduled messages
  • Message editing and deletion
  • Search across chats, files, and links
  • AI assistant inside every chat
  • AI-generated stickers/images
  • Conversation summaries
  • Smart suggestions and automation

AI Functions

Writing help, context help, action help, and safety help.

Smart Replies

  • Smart reply suggestions

  • More professional / More friendly / More confident / More empathetic

Summarize

  • Chat summary / Unread summary / Decision summary / Action-item summary

Visual AI

Voice AI

  • Speech-to-text / Text-to-speech / Voice conversation mode / Call summaries

Social Intelligence

  • Suggest response timing / Detect unanswered important messages

Group AI

Feature Backlog

Proactive workflow and memory for an agentic direction.

  • AI memory per contact or workspace
  • Conversation goals, such as “help me close this sale” or “help plan this trip”
  • Multi-agent chat modes, such as researcher, writer, scheduler
  • Shared AI for groups with permissions
  • Plugin/tool system
  • Knowledge-base grounding for teams
  • Conversation sentiment dashboard
  • Smart moderation for communities
  • AI-generated meeting minutes after voice/video calls
  • Cross-app action center from chat, such as calendar, tasks, docs, commerce, support

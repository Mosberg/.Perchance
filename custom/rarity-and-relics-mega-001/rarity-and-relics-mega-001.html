Rarity and Relics

Gear up, take a risk, and uncover legendary gear in every adventure!

A Minecraft 1.20.1 Fabric mod that brings depth and unpredictability to weapons, tools, and equipment.

Key Features

Dynamic Item Rarity

Every weapon, tool, or piece of equipment crafted or found in loot is automatically assigned a random rarity tier.

Random Buffs & Debuffs

Items gain a selection of buffs and debuffs (stat modifiers) upon creation or discovery. Each rarity tier has its own pool of possible modifiers that affect attributes like attack damage, speed, and durability.

Loot & Craft Integration

Rarity and stat effects are applied to crafted items, chest loot, and mob drops. Every acquisition of equipment is a unique experience!

Fully Data-Driven

Both rarities and buffs/debuffs are defined using JSON files. Easy to adjust, balance, or expand without code changes. Perfect for custom modpacks and themed servers.

Persistent, Visible Properties

Each item's properties are permanently stored in NBT, clearly displayed in tooltips, and preserved when moved, dropped, or traded.

Extensibility

Designed for compatibility and expansion. Works with most loot and equipment mods. Easy to add new effects and modify rarity weights through configuration.

Rarity Tiers

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythical → Relic

📖 How to Use the Dokou AI Generator

The generator combines several menus and content sources (imports) to generate professional images. Here's how to make use of each tool:

  • 📝 Prompt: Write a description of the image you want in the Prompt box. You can write in Arabic or English, but English gives more accurate results.
  • 🚫 Negative Prompt: Put here what you do not want to appear in the image (e.g., lowres, blurry, bad anatomy).
  • ⚡ AI Tools: The Enhance button auto-improves the description, Suggest proposes descriptions, and Contrast adjusts the contrast.
  • 🎯 Presets: The Outfit, Style, Character, Actions, BG, Cam, Tags... buttons add ready-made tags from imported content lists (char-list, style-list, poses-list, world-list, effects-list...).
  • ⚙️ Settings: Click Settings to control Aspect Ratio, Seed, Guidance Scale, and more.
  • 🔄 Refresh / Reroll: If you don't like the result, click Refresh to generate a new image with the same description.
  • 💾 Save Images: After generation, you can save the image locally or upload it to the gallery.
  • 🪄 Regeneration Studio: Click the Studio button () under any generated image to open an advanced editor. From there you can: edit the prompt, seed, and guidance scale directly; change the aspect ratio (Portrait / Landscape / Square); generate multiple variations in separate tabs; download images with embedded metadata (prompt + seed); remove the background automatically; copy the seed to reproduce results; and zoom into image info. It's the fastest way to iterate on a result you almost like.

📦 Generator Contents (Imports)

The generator uses ready-made lists that can be referenced directly:

  • char — Character list
  • style — Art style list
  • outfit — Clothing and fashion
  • poses — Body poses
  • world — Backgrounds and worlds
  • effects — Visual effects
  • anime / cosp / game — Anime/Cosplay/Game styles

🎨 Style Preview

Art styles control the overall look of the image. Here are examples of how to use them in the description:

  • Anime/Manga: anime style, cel shading, vibrant colors
  • Realistic: photorealistic, 8k, highly detailed, soft lighting
  • Watercolor: watercolor painting, soft edges, pastel tones
  • Oil/Classic: oil painting, baroque style, dramatic lighting
  • Cyberpunk: cyberpunk, neon lights, dark atmosphere, futuristic
  • 3D Render: 3d render, octane render, cinematic, volumetric light

Use the Style button in the Presets section to add style tags automatically, or type them manually in the description.

🪄 Tip: Iterate on styles in the Studio

When you open the Regeneration Studio on an image, you can edit the prompt to swap style keywords (e.g. change anime style to watercolor painting) and hit Generate to instantly compare variations side-by-side in separate tabs — without losing your original. This is the fastest way to find the style that fits your idea.

⚠️ Rules & Policies

⛔ Most Important Rule — Limit on Similar Images

  • Maximum of two (2) images for the same content in the gallery. Do not post more than two images with the same composition/subject.
  • Posting many similar images may result in a posting ban. Excessive repetition of the same image or identical prompts is considered spam and may get your account banned.
  • Vary your prompts, styles, and characters between posts to ensure content diversity.
  • Appropriate content: Do not post content that violates platform rules or is illegal.
  • Respect ownership: Do not claim others' images as your own, and do not use clearly protected trademarks.
  • No spam: Do not post too quickly or repeatedly over short intervals.
  • Accurate description: When posting, write an honest description of the image.
  • Tool usage: The tools (Enhance, Suggest, Presets) are meant to improve your own prompt — do not share their raw output as final content.
  • Studio iterations: The Regeneration Studio lets you create many variations quickly. This is great for finding the best result, but remember the two-similar-images rule above still applies — do not upload every variation to the gallery. Pick your best one or two only.

By using this generator, you agree to follow these rules. Violations may result in content removal or account ban.

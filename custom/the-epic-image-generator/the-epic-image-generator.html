      ❓ Slash Commands

      Text-transform commands — put them BEFORE your message

      • /uwu — ends your sentence with UWU, Nya~, (⁠≧⁠﹏⁠≦⁠), ~, >:3, or :3
      • /mock — mAkEs ThE cOmMeNt LoOk LiKe ThIs
      • /gothic — makes the text gothic font
      • /italic — outputs italic letters
      • /bold — makes your letters bold
      • /small — makes your letters small
      • /smol — same as /small but even smaller
      • /reverse — makes the letters opposite (A becomes Z)
      • /script — outputs a script 𝗙𝗢𝗡𝗧
      • /frizzle — outputs frizzle text
      • /bode — outputs bode text
      • /leetspeak — m4k35 y0ur 73x7 l00k l1k3 7h15
      • /emoji — shows how to suggest emojis
      • /quote — "Does this to your comment."
      • /d4 — outputs a number 1-4
      • /d6 — outputs a number 1-6
      • /d8 — outputs a number 1-8
      • /d10 — outputs a number 1-10
      • /d12 — outputs a number 1-12
      • /d20 — outputs a number 1-20
      NOTE: 𝗡𝗘𝗩𝗘𝗥 put your /command at the end of your input or it'll 𝗡𝗢𝗧 work.
      ✅ CORRECT: /uwu Hello World → Hello World (⁠≧⁠﹏⁠≦⁠)
      ❌ WRONG: Hello World /uwu → outputs exactly what you typed
